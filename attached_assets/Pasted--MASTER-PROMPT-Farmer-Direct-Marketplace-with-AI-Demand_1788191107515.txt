# MASTER PROMPT — Farmer Direct Marketplace with AI Demand Forecasting

You are a senior full-stack software engineer and UI/UX designer. Build a complete, working, presentation-ready web application from end to end based on the following problem statement.

## 1. PROJECT TITLE

**FarmDirect — Direct Farmer-to-Customer Digital Marketplace**

### Problem Statement

Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.

The goal is to create a digital marketplace that:

* Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
* Allows farmers to sell products directly.
* Allows customers to purchase products directly from farmers.
* Provides basic logistics and delivery support.
* Uses AI/data-driven demand forecasting to help farmers decide what to grow or stock.
* Uses route optimization concepts to improve delivery efficiency.

### Expected Benefits

* Better prices for farmers.
* Lower prices for consumers.
* Reduced supply-chain inefficiencies.
* Better demand visibility for farmers.
* Improved delivery management.
